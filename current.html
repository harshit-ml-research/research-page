The research question

How reliably can these models call tools: choose a tool, form arguments, use the result, and stop? The score is tool-calling behavior on a frozen scenario set: tool choice, arguments, use of the result, and stopping.

What is decided

  1. Evaluation firstAbout 100 tool-calling scenarios. Param2 plus a couple of models in the same range.
  2. Training data secondSupervised traces from several teachers, aimed at tool calling. The teacher list and the trace count per teacher are still a plan, not a launch.
  3. Everything else laterTool catalog, scenario source, budgets, and serving choices stay open until they are written down as a run.

No new scores yet. This page states the investigation. It does not report a result.

Expected research artifacts

A frozen scenario manifest, raw generations, parsed tool calls, and a score that separates a valid call from a correct downstream outcome. Training traces, when they exist, will be a separate release from this evaluation set.
